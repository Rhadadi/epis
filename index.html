How do you know?

  1. THE QUESTION HOW DO YOU KNOW?

    a free course in the theory of knowledge

  2. You read a headline: “People who drink two cups of coffee a day live 18% longer.”

    Do you believe it?

  3. Your first guess

    Did the coffee do it?

    • Yes, coffee makes you live longer
    • Maybe something else explains it
    • No, it proves nothing

  4. That question has a name

    Epistemology is the study of how we know: knowledge, belief, evidence, justification and rationality.

    It sits under every other field, because every field makes claims to know.
