# Pronunciation fixes for the Persian narration.
#
# One entry per line: the word, a tab, and its pronunciation in IPA between
# slashes. Only the sounds of Persian may be used: a e ɑ i o u, b p t d k ɡ q ʔ
# f v s z ʃ ʒ x h tʃ dʒ m n l r j, and ˈ before the stressed syllable.
#
# A single Persian word is compiled into espeak-ng's Persian dictionary, so the
# fix also reaches the word with suffixes and the ezafe (گتیه، گتیه‌ای، گتیهٔ).
# Latin-script terms, and Persian compounds written with a space or ZWNJ, are
# replaced by the given phonemes wherever they appear exactly as written.
#
# Latin-script words that are not listed here are said with espeak-ng's English
# pronunciation, mapped to the nearest Persian sounds.
