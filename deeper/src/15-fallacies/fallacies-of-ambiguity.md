> **In short.** An argument needs its important terms to retain the meanings on which the inference depends. Ambiguity becomes a reasoning defect when a shift supplies an appearance of support. Clarifying a word can reveal two different arguments, each needing its own defence. Similar mistakes move between individuals and groups or between one shared thing and many different things. Rewrite the claim precisely before deciding whether it follows.

## Re-learn

### Keep the middle term stable

Consider this invented argument: every secure investment protects your principal; this company offers a secure investment because its website uses encryption; therefore the investment protects your principal. Financial security and connection security are different properties. Encryption might protect communication with a company that still loses investors' money.

Replacing the two occurrences with *principal-protecting* and *encrypted* reveals the gap. Equivocation is this illegitimate shift of meaning during reasoning. The IEP entry's examples show why reusing a spelling is insufficient to preserve the connection. [@fallacy-iep, Equivocation]

The repair needs a further premise about the actual investment terms, the provider's reliability, and the risk of default. Clarification has located the issue; it has not proved the investment bad. Compare [the fallacy fallacy](deeper:15-fallacies/the-fallacy-fallacy).

### Structure can be ambiguous too

Imagine an administrator reporting that the school interviewed the students with complaints. This could identify which students were interviewed or describe what accompanied the interviews. If an argument depends on a particular reading, the wording alone may not establish it. The IEP account calls grammatical ambiguity *amphiboly*, distinguishing it from a word's multiple meanings. [@fallacy-iep, Amphiboly]

Ask which people and which action the sentence describes. A clearer version might say that only students who had submitted complaints were interviewed. That supplies a definite claim to check against the selection records. The purpose is to make disagreement possible on a shared proposition, rather than to eliminate every possible ambiguity from ordinary speech.

## Beyond the chapter

### Which level carries the property?

A collection of cheap items need not be cheap in total. A costly collection need not contain only costly items. These are constructed examples of composition and division: wrongly transferring a characteristic from members to the whole or in the reverse direction. The IEP entries describe both patterns. [@fallacy-iep, Composition, Division]

Specify the property and its aggregation rule. If every item costs less than a pound, a thousand items can still cost hundreds of pounds. If every item is made entirely of steel, the items taken together are steel objects. The form of a part-to-whole inference alone does not establish its failure; the relevant property determines what follows.

Another shift concerns quantity. Every student may have a supervisor without there being one supervisor for all students. The IEP calls the confusion between these quantifier orders a quantifier-shift fallacy. Writing down who is assigned to whom exposes the difference. [@fallacy-iep, Quantifier Shift]

### Context is part of what was said

A quotation can accurately reproduce words while misrepresenting their speaker's position. In an invented report, a sentence announcing that a proposed explanation seems impossible might introduce the next paragraph's solution. Presenting it as the report's settled verdict would distort its role. The IEP treatment connects context-stripping to straw-man argumentation. [@fallacy-iep, Quoting out of Context]

Preserve qualifications, the target of the claim, and whether the author is asserting a view or considering an objection. Then ask whether the clarified argument supports the clarified conclusion. See [language and ambiguity](04-language-concepts-and-definitions.md#ambiguity) for the background distinctions; this page concerns what goes wrong when those distinctions matter to an inference.
