Famous books to read alongside the quests. We never copy them: borrow them from a library or a friend, then use the questions to talk.

6–9 · Unit 1

Nate the Great

Marjorie Weinman Sharmat, 1972

A boy detective who never guesses: he looks for clues until he knows.

Talk about it

  • Find one moment when Nate could have guessed. What did he do instead?
  • Which clue helped Nate the most? Why was it a good clue?
  • Was there a clue that could have tricked him?

5–8 · Unit 1

Ada Twist, Scientist

Andrea Beaty, 2016

A girl who asks "why?" about everything, and then tests her ideas to find out.

Talk about it

  • What did Ada want to know?
  • How did she check her ideas?
  • What would you like to find out, and how could you check?

7–10 · Unit 1

The True Story of the Three Little Pigs

Jon Scieszka, 1989

The wolf tells his side of the story. Should we believe him?

Talk about it

  • What does the wolf say really happened?
  • What evidence does he give? Is it good evidence?
  • How could you find out which story is true?

12+ · Unit 1

Sophie's World

Jostein Gaarder, 1991

A girl gets mysterious letters asking "Who are you?" and "Where does the world come from?" — a novel that walks through the history of philosophy.

Talk about it

  • Which question in the letters would you most like to answer?
  • What does Sophie believe at the start that she later doubts?
  • How does she decide whom to trust?
