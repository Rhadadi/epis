---
title: The Fox and the Sweet Grapes
---

@shot s01

@grandma: Here is a very old story. A storyteller called Aesop told it more than two thousand years ago.

Once upon a time, a hungry fox trotted past a garden wall.

@shot s02

High above him, on a vine, hung big, round, purple grapes.

@fox: Yum! Those grapes look so sweet!

@shot s03

The fox jumped. He jumped again. He jumped and jumped. But the grapes were too high.

@shot s04

At last he sat down, puffing and panting. Then he lifted his nose and said:

@fox: Hmph! Those grapes are sour anyway. I don't want them!

@shot s05

A little goat was eating grass nearby. Her name was Little Grape.

@grape: What makes you say that?

@shot s06

@fox: Well… they are very high up. And I am very tired from all that jumping.

@grape: But those are reasons about you, not about the grapes. Did you taste one?

@fox: No. I never did.

@shot s07

Little Grape found a ladder by the wall. She held it steady while the fox climbed up and picked one grape. He popped it into his mouth.

@fox: Oh! It's sweet!

@shot s08

The fox said sorry to the grapes, and the two friends ate them in the sun.

@grandma: The fox did not know the grapes were sour. He said "sour" because he could not reach them. So when someone says something, ask: what makes you say that?
