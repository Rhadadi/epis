---
title: Shangul, Mangul and Little Grape
---

@shot s01

@grandma: Here is a very old story. Children in Iran have heard it for a long, long time.

Once upon a time, a mother goat lived in a little house near the woods. She had three kids. Their names were Shangul, Mangul and Little Grape.

@shot s02

One morning, Mother Goat had to go and find grass.

@mother: Lock the door when I go. Open it only for me. When I come back, I will sing our secret song.

@mother: And remember: I have a soft voice and white feet. The wolf has a rough voice and black feet.

@kids: We will remember!

@shot s03

Soon there was a knock at the door. Knock, knock, knock!

@kids: Who is it?

@wolf: Open the door, my dears. It's your mother!

The voice was rough and loud. Shangul looked at Mangul.

@shangul: You are not our mother. Our mother has a soft voice. You are the wolf!

@shot s04

The wolf ran off. He ate a big spoon of honey to make his voice sweet. Then he came back.

@wolf: Open the door, my dears. It's your mother!

Now the voice was soft and sweet. Mangul was not sure. "Show us your foot," he said. A big black foot came under the door.

@mangul: You are not our mother. Our mother has white feet. You are the wolf!

@shot s05

The wolf ran to the baker. He rubbed his feet in white flour. Then he came back again.

@wolf: Open the door, my dears. It's your mother! Look at my white feet.

The voice was soft. The foot was white. Shangul and Mangul ran to open the door.

@shot s06

@grape: Wait! A wolf can make his voice soft. A wolf can make his feet white. But only Mother knows our secret song. Sing it, please!

The wolf did not know the song. He hummed. He coughed. He sang "la la la".

@shot s07

@kids: That is not our song. You are the wolf! Go away!

The wolf was so cross that he ran far into the woods, and he never came back.

@shot s08

At last, Mother Goat came home. She sang the secret song, every word. The kids opened the door and gave her a big hug.

@mother: How did you know it was really me?

@grape: A voice can be copied. A white foot can be faked. But nobody else knows our song!

@grandma: In some old books the wolf is very scary. In our story, he just goes away. Now, Ava, Nima: how did Little Grape know?
