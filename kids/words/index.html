Picture dictionary

Every thinking word from the lessons, in plain words.

believe

To believe something is to think it is true. A belief can be right or wrong.

evidence

Evidence is a clue that helps show whether something is true.

fake

To fake a clue is to make it look true when it isn't, like the wolf's white feet.

guess

A guess is an answer you give when you don't have a good reason yet.

hope

To hope is to want something to be true. Wanting it doesn't make it true.

know

To know something is to be right about it for a good reason, not just by luck.

lucky guess

A lucky guess is right, but only by chance. Being right by luck isn't knowing.

reason

A reason is what makes you think something: the "because" behind it.
